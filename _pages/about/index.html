Mehedi Hasan Abid, a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Regina, Canada, working under the supervision of Dr. JingTao Yao. My research interests focus on Three-Way Clustering, Explainable Deep Learning, and Adversarial Robustness, with applications in agriculture, healthcare, and sustainable AI systems.

Previously, I served as a Lecturer and Instructional Designer at Daffodil International University (DIU), Bangladesh. My work bridges research, teaching, and real-world deployment of intelligent systems.

I am **Dm. Mehedi Hasan Abid**, a PhD student in the [Department of Computer Science](https://www.uregina.ca/science/cs/) at the **University of Regina, Canada**, working under the supervision of [Dr. JingTao Yao](https://www2.cs.uregina.ca/~jtyao/). My research interests focus on Machine Learning and Explainable AI (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explainable_artificial_intelligence).

Previously, I served as a [Lecturer and Sr. Administrative Officer](https://daffodilvarsity.edu.bd/admin-detail/abid-663f56f16600) at [Daffodil International University (DIU), Bangladesh](https://daffodilvarsity.edu.bd/). I taught undergraduate courses, supervised thesis projects, and supported academic program development at the Human Resource Development Institute (HRDI). Being part of the Administrative jobs I have trained more than 5 faculty, 15+ department and 300+ faculty members in the domain of Online Teaching, How to teach better online. My work bridges **research, teaching, and real-world deployment** of intelligent systems.

I am open to any collaborative work in my selective domain. If you are interested, send me an email.

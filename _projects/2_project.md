## Overview

DailySpeak is an MVP prototype designed to help learners practice simple English for real-life daily situations. The platform focuses on practical communication needs such as transport, shopping, health, school, work, and home.

## Key Features

- Real-life speaking practice for everyday situations  
- Scenario categories such as Transport, Shopping, Health, School, Work, and Home  
- Simple learner-friendly interface  
- Bengali-supported navigation for early users  
- User-submitted situation feature with moderator review  

## My Role

I led the project idea, design direction, content structure, and development of the prototype. I also worked on validating the concept through early user feedback and improving the scenarios based on real communication needs.

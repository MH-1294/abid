## Overview

AgroVision AI is an end-to-end artificial intelligence system designed for crop disease detection and severity-aware decision support in agriculture. The project focuses on developing lightweight and explainable models that can operate under real-world constraints such as limited data, resource constraints, and varying environmental conditions.

## Key Contributions

- Crop disease classification using lightweight convolutional neural networks  
- Severity-aware analysis to support early-stage intervention and treatment decisions  
- Integration of explainability techniques (Grad-CAM) for transparent predictions  
- Design considerations for real-world deployment (mobile/drone-based usage)
